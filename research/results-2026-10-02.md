window of the decoded sequence; tokens = windows not inside one match whose source window lies inside one record (such a

## I2. Self-repair of bit flips

The 16 MiB chr1 slice of T-H4 in the open profile (4 469 784 B, md5 84eba878...). `research/selfheal.cpp`: the decoder
names the failing unit (literal chunk, token chunk, block, layout), every bit of its bytes is flipped in turn, a probe
re-decodes only what the bit touches, XXH3 of the output is the oracle. found = the repaired archive == the original
bytes; no locator = every unit decodes and only the oracle fails (bytes stored raw). 16 copies at a time.

| flips | copies | found | not found | no locator | false | harmless | probes per copy (mean) | CPU per copy, s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1000 | 990 | 0 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 72 142 | 1.331 |
| 2 | 100 | 93 | 7 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 148 579 | 2.624 |

With two flips a probe that makes its unit and that unit's blocks decode is kept and the next failing unit is searched
(the oracle runs at the end); the 7 not found: both flips in one unit or the first fix not the original bit.
